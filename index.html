Meet us(3)

KPFounder and investor

K.P.

Commercial strategy and raising money

K.P. has done the hard commercial parts of deep tech. She co-founded a medical device company that got CE and FDA clearance, spent four years as an investor with two portfolio companies acquired, one of them by Apple, and mentors founders at Zinc and the Oxford Moonshot programme. She's the one who'll tell you straight whether you're really ready to raise.

CKFractional COO

C.K.

Product, strategy and operations

C.K. sorts out product and operations. She built an innovation lab, ran an accelerator at Vodafone, and now does product and strategy consulting across fintech, automotive and health tech, with ed tech before that. She's also a fractional COO and leads a health-tech accelerator. She's good at turning a messy early team into something that runs, and at telling you where AI genuinely helps and where it's a distraction.

GWFractional CMO

G.W.

Marketing and growth

G.W. does marketing for complicated B2B companies, and has done for more than fifteen years, at startups and at enterprise scale. Fractional CMO, five years running her own shop, marketing through four acquisitions and two raises. She works from strategy through to delivery, so what you get is a growth engine that actually runs rather than a deck describing one.
